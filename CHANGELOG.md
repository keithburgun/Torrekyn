# Torrekin changelog

Newest first. The version shows on the title screen.

## 0.11.7 — 2026-11-16
- New Greater Trinket, New Power (4 Gold): Discover one of 3 Hero Powers to replace yours. Whatever your old power gave you stays (Andromeda's Pumpkin, gained cards, stats).

## 0.11.6 — 2026-11-16
- Horseman comes back from Reborn with its full stats. New Greater Trinket, Another Chance (4 Gold): the first time you run out of Health, your Health goes back to 10 and you get 10 Gold next turn. Trinket prices are much bigger. Ash is a 3/3. ('Look at army' no longer sits on the sound button.)

## 0.11.5 — 2026-11-16
- Bill Milton (card): End of Turn: gain +1/+1 for each maximum Gold you have (Golden +2/+2).

## 0.11.4 — 2026-11-16
- Hovering a card zooms it in after a quarter of a second (and only if the mouse is still on it), and never while there's a button under the mouse, so cards dealt out of the Refresh button no longer pop up.

## 0.11.3 — 2026-11-16
- Zoomed cards show the cards their text names beside them after half a second (Bulldozer shows the Red Gem, Organizer the Potato Gem, Life the Pink Gem, Secret the Forest Abomination...), in the shop and in battle.

## 0.11.2 — 2026-11-16
- Therapist: Battlecry: give a unit Taunt and +12 Health (Golden +24). Iron Bracer: Start of Combat: give 2 random friendly units Divine Shield.

## 0.11.1 — 2026-11-16
- Iron Bracer: Start of Combat: give your left-most unit Taunt and Reborn.

## 0.11.0 — 2026-11-15
- Battles play on the shop screen: your army, hero, hand, gold and leaderboard stay where they are; your opponent's army takes the Tavern's row and their hero the Tavern's circle; Speed / Pause / Skip replace Begin Battle. You can hover your hero, the leaderboard and units during the battle; nothing can be bought, moved or played until it's over. No more intro and wipe screens. Purple Gem: give a unit +1/+1 (for good; Golden +2/+2), and Reborn for 1 turn.

## 0.10.4 — 2026-11-15
- Omar Evans no longer starts with Venomous. Organizer: Taunt. Deathrattle: Summon a Potato Gem (Golden: a Golden one), whose Battlecry happens as it arrives. Green Ninja is 5/2. Every Gem is a 1/1 (Blue, Red and Potato Gem were bigger). New Lesser Trinket, Rattler (2 Gold): Start of Combat: give 4 random friendly units 'Deathrattle: Summon a random Tier 1 unit' (shown on them in battle).

## 0.10.3 — 2026-11-15
- Larva is a one-shot: after its turns in hand it gains its Golden Tier 5 Cloister unit and stays a Larva, marked (Done!) on the card and Done! on its counter.

## 0.10.2 — 2026-11-15
- Shield: a 1/3 with Taunt. Battlecry: give a friendly unit (you choose) Taunt; Golden gives one more random unit Taunt. Storyteller: selling it gains a random Cloister unit (up to your Tavern Tier; Golden two). Every hero starts with 4 more Health.

## 0.10.1 — 2026-11-15
- Battle damage is now the winner's Tavern Tier + 1 for each of their surviving units (at most 12). Tier stars show on the winner's hero again. 'Maximum Damage' achievement: deal 12 in one battle.

## 0.10.0 — 2026-11-15
- Slots no longer have upgrades: the slot system is gone, along with its labels and help text. Rewritten without slots: Message, Front and Center (new picture), Remember, Commitment, Gelf Lanz, Architect, Patchwork, Builder, Devotion (a free Lesser Trinket), The Mayor (50/5), Cursed Vines, Elder (1 Attack), Andromeda Robin, Owl, Paradox and the Potato Gem. Trinkets: Iron Bracer and Phoenix Feather give your left-most unit Taunt / Reborn at Start of Combat; Bannerlord's Standard gives your units +3/+3 at Start of Combat. Two slot achievements replaced (Built to Last, Collector); three achievements retuned for the halved Health and new damage.

## 0.9.1 — 2026-11-15
- Pink Gem: Battlecry: give a unit Divine Shield for this turn (Golden: and 1 other random unit). Red Gem gives +3 Attack (was +3 of a random stat). Zoomed and Discover cards show the Tier as stars, like every other card.

## 0.9.0 — 2026-11-15
- Left and Right Twinbot are now 1/2 (no more 0-Attack units). Refresh deals the new cards out one by one from the Refresh button. Longbow and Sniper fire a bullet with a muzzle flash and a gunshot. Units with Avenge show their progress (e.g. 1/3) over their picture in battle. Trinket and Discover choices can be tucked away ('Look at army') to look at your board, then brought back; other actions wait until you choose. Your hand is shown beside your hero during battles: cards gained arrive with a sparkle, cards summoned from it lift away. 'Triple!' and gem/counter badges sit just above the Tier instead of covering it.

## 0.8.0 — 2026-11-12
- Hero Health halved; battle damage is now 1 + 1 per surviving unit. New Commitment: Battlecry: give your left-most slot +20 Health per Azure unit. Chaos counts Battlecry units played (not extra triggers). Tinker flashes, then the shop unit it upgraded flashes as its keyword lands. Hero powers going off get a burst of light, a bigger label and their own chime (e.g. Derby Pocket's extra Battlecry). Repeated Battlecries play twice as fast. Taunt shields stick out more. A slot's bonus shows under the unit's Attack and Health so keyword coins don't cover it. Extra keywords on the big card show as coloured italics. Banners no longer show a number over the Tier. Bots: even the weakest persona now plays with real skill (weakest bots averaged 12/11 units by turn 14; now 34/40); new players start mid-ladder (1400) and existing ratings below that move up; persona ratings re-measured; card values re-measured.

## 0.7.0 — 2026-11-12
- Smarter bots (+99 Elo vs the old Expert): play the best card in hand when the army is full, value units that grow, better Gem/Banner/Mod/Abandon placement, hold Larva/Green Ninja/Scout, sell Treasure and Message well, faster leveling, pickier rerolls; card values re-measured with Tier 6. May's Root Access costs 2. Fixes: Left Twinbot's Rally gave Health too (Attack only now); Scout Discovered a Tier too low (now shows its current Tier). New ability test checks every card does something.

## 0.6.0 — 2026-11-12
- Damage dealt is now 2 + 2 for each surviving unit (the base 2 shows as stars on the winner's hero).
- Shield (Neutral) no longer destroys itself.
- Pickaxe is now Gambler: Battlecry: Gain 1 free Refresh (Golden 2), with new art. The Refresh button shows free Refreshes.
- Summit Key: In two turns, Discover a Tier 6 unit.
- Fix: Gems played by an effect (Invisible Hand, Bulldozer) skipped your left-most unit. In battle, each Gem now shows as its own step with a red gem flying to every unit it buffs.
- Banners show their current (improved) amount on the card.
- Buff floats: a card's stats now change as each float lands, instead of jumping to the total first.
- The last-battles popup on the leaderboard now shows above your army.
- Keyword badges (Reborn purple, Venomous green, Poisonous lime, Windfury blue) are chunky coloured coins in a row under the tribe banner.

## 0.5.5 — 2026-11-10
- Fix: extra Battlecry triggers multiplied instead of adding up. Derby Pocket + Crown of Echoes now gives 3 triggers (was 4); Gem Wizard + Crown on a Red Gem gives 3 (was 4).

## 0.5.4 — 2026-11-10
- Fix: Left Twinbot (0 Attack) never attacked, so its Rally never happened. Units with a Rally now attack even at 0 Attack.

## 0.5.3 — 2026-11-10
- The results screen no longer shows a zoomed card left open behind it.

## 0.5.2 — 2026-11-10
- Desktop: the short tap-safety delay on Buy / Play / Sell now applies only to touch taps, so quick mouse clicks are never ignored.

## 0.5.1 — 2026-11-10
- Phones: tapping a unit can't instantly sell it any more. When the big card has to cover the card you tapped, its Sell / Play / Buy buttons sit above it instead of right under your finger, and those buttons ignore taps for the first moment after a card opens (they fade in as they arm).

## 0.5.0 — 2026-11-10
- Gelf Lanz's Inspire is now an active power: 3 Gold, gain an Azure unit; it costs 1 less each time you buy an Azure unit.
- Interface back to the smooth, rounded look by default (Pixel stays in Options → Interface). The Tavern buttons are rounded pink buttons like Begin Battle with Keith's black pixel icons; the fonts and the red Health orbs stay.
- Leaderboard Health shows a small red orb instead of a heart.
